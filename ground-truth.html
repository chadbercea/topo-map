    OPERATION DRY CREEK

    Three hostile cells are operating on the south rim of the Snake River canyon. Team ALPHA, five operators, will find them, neutralize them and get out. You have five minutes.

    You're their intel. Reports stream in live, sometimes several at once, and each one goes stale after 40 seconds. The list holds five; the rest wait in line, and each new one pings its spot on the map. Some are true, some are false, and true ones drift as the cells move. Work the pile.

    • Send a report and ALPHA moves on it. They act on what you send, and nothing else.
    • Read each report before you trust it. The grade is the source's track record: A is right 90% of the time, E only 15%. Reports that agree with each other usually point at something real; false ones stand alone.
    • Sort the list by newest, grade, agreement or what's about to expire, and pin ★ the ones you're watching to the top.
    • Check a report when you need to be sure. The analyst takes eight seconds each and works a queue of three.
    • Tap the map to sweep. It checks the reports around it and reveals any cell there. Tap a revealed cell to send ALPHA its live position.
    • Keys: ↑↓ select, S send, C check, D discard, P pin, 1–4 sort, Space pause. Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom.
