PMC War Room · project overview

A browser game where you’re the support, not the soldier.

You are Castle House, a private military company’s war room. A five-man team is on the ground recovering a downed drone’s black box. You never control them: you task a satellite, buy strikes and keep them fed with intel, and they make their own decisions. Designed by Chad Bercea, built with Claude Code.

The game

PMC War Room: Operation Black Box

Scan the map with ORION-3, keep the team’s intel gauge full, change the odds with sniper, drone and helicopter strikes, and get everyone out before the contract window closes. Then read the P&L, buy upgrades and go again.

  • Desktop & phone
  • Mouse or touch
  • About 10 minutes a contract

How it’s made

One designer, one AI coding agent, one ticket at a time.

  1. Play itChad plays the live build and says what works and what doesn’t.
  2. Write it downEach note becomes a Linear ticket, with the decision and the reason.
  3. Build itClaude Code builds it on a branch, with tests and screenshots at desktop and phone sizes.
  4. Ship itCI checks it, the pull request merges itself, and production updates.
